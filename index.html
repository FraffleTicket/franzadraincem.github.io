About Me

From bedside care to remote support

I started my career as a Registered Nurse, working directly with patients in a fast-paced public hospital setting. That environment taught me how to stay organized under pressure, document accurately, communicate clearly with patients and colleagues, and follow structured protocols without cutting corners.

When I moved into Medical Virtual Assistance, I found those same habits translated directly: careful intake of information, precise documentation in electronic health records, and a calm, professional approach to patient and client communication. I've since built on that foundation with administrative VA work and hands-on experience in social media and graphic design, developed through student organization work and personal projects.

Today, I'm growing my career in remote work that draws on all three parts of my background — healthcare knowledge, administrative reliability, and creative skill — rather than treating them as separate paths.
